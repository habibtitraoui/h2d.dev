NO MAGIC WORDS. JUST A SOLID PROCESS.

Start with the
actual problem.

Good software usually starts before the first line of code. I like to get clear on what’s getting in the way, then build the smallest useful thing that fixes it.

That sound like your kind of project? ↗
01

Talk it through

What’s happening today? Who’s doing the work? What would “better” actually look like?

⌕
02

Pick the right first version

We agree on the important bits, skip the nice-to-haves for now and make a plan you can follow.

◫
03

Build, test, adjust

Real progress, shown early. You can try the thing before we call it finished.

Deployment, handover and the practical next steps. No mysterious disappearing act.
